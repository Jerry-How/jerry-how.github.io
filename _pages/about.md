I'm Jiawei Hou (侯甲慰), a fourth-year Ph.D. student from EIC, [Huazhong University of Science and Technology](https://www.hust.edu.cn/), advised by [Prof. Peng Yang](https://faculty.hust.edu.cn/pyang/en). I received my bachelor's degree from [Hainan University](https://www.hainanu.edu.cn/) in 2022. I am currently an exchange student at A\*STAR, [Institute of Advanced Intelligence and Computing (IAIC)](https://asef.a-star.edu.sg/institutes/iaic), Singapore, supervised by [Prof. Sumei Sun](https://www.a-star.edu.sg/i2r/about-i2r/i2r-management/sun-sumei) and [Prof. Gary Lee](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=-k6WQI0AAAAJ&hl=en). My research interests include mobile edge computing 📱📱, vehicular networks ⭐⭐, and cooperative perception 🚗🚗.

My email is: jerry\_hou@hust.edu.cn.
